# JAR – JPEG Auto Repair

**Grey areas, shifted bands, wrong colours?** JAR finds where a corrupt JPEG broke, aligns the picture data back into place, and corrects colour shifts—offline on your Windows PC.

Built by [Joep van Steen (DiskTuna)](https://disktuna.com), drawing on 20+ years of hands-on photo and data recovery experience.

---

## Key Features
* **Automatic Repair:** Detects damaged MCU/tile boundaries, shifts misaligned bands back into place, and normalises colour casts in one click.
* **Assisted Mode:** Step in manually with pixel-level alignment (`+`/`−`), brightness/colour sliders, and visual rulers for tricky areas like clear skies or plain walls.
* **Header Repair:** Rebuilds damaged JPEG headers using an intact reference photo from the same camera.
* **RAW JPEG Extraction:** Pulls the largest embedded JPEG (with original Exif metadata) out of intact or damaged RAW files (`CR2`, `CR3`, `NEF`, `ARW`, `RAF`, `ORF`, `RW2`, `DNG`, etc.).
* **Built-In Diagnostics:** Inspect files before repairing with a byte map, entropy graph, and byte histogram.
* **100% Offline & Non-Destructive:** Your photos never leave your PC, and original files are never modified.
